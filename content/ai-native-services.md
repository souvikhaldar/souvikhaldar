I help founders and companies redesign their businesses for the AI-native era.

Not chatbots bolted onto old workflows. Not another AI feature inside an existing product. I build the operational system itself, where AI agents execute the repeatable work, humans supervise the exceptions, and the entire company becomes faster, cheaper, more transparent, and auditable by default.

## What this service does

- Build new companies from scratch as AI-native operations.
- Re-engineer existing companies to reduce workforce dependency without losing capability.
- Automate repetitive workflows across operations, compliance, support, sales, and back-office.
- Stand up transparent dashboards, audit trails, and institutional memory as first-class outputs of the workflow.
- Deploy AI agents for execution, reporting, coordination, and decision support.
- Convert traditional service-heavy businesses into scalable AI-native systems.

## Proof of concept: Hornet

The first live implementation of this model is [Hornet](https://www.hornet.technology), my own company in cyber intelligence and crypto forensics.

Hornet has a real codebase, real customers, and a deliberately lean operating footprint. It is being rebuilt and operated as an AI-native company, with AI agents handling:

- On-demand investigation support.
- Crypto tracing workflows.
- Intelligence report generation.
- Customer onboarding.
- Sales and proposal preparation.
- Compliance documentation.
- Internal operations and case management.

This is the live proof that a complex, high-trust company in a regulated domain can be operated and scaled with minimal workforce dependency.
