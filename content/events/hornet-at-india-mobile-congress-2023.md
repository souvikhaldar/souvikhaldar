Hornet was selected by the Department of Information Technology & Electronics, Government of West Bengal, to represent Bengal's technology ecosystem at India Mobile Congress 2023 in New Delhi.

IMC brought together ministries, major technology companies, startups, and builders under one roof. The event was inaugurated by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, whose address also emphasized the importance of cybersecurity in India's technology progress.

For Hornet, the event was a chance to present work around cryptocurrency investigation, dark web intelligence, wallet analysis, illicit-use tracking, and secret-management risk across the web.

The broader message was clear: India’s technology ecosystem is growing quickly, and cybercrime investigation infrastructure has to grow with it.
