I am truly thankful to the Ministry of Defence for inviting me, along with other cybersecurity-focused startups, to share feedback on how emerging companies like Hornet can contribute to strengthening global safety and security.

It was inspiring to see the openness of our bureaucrats toward creating policies that uplift startups, enhance cybersecurity posture, and drive efficiency across the ecosystem.

This change in mindset reflects India's growing commitment to cyber resilience. I am confident that the days ahead will be marked by stronger, more robust, and forward-looking frameworks.

Looking forward to contributing further to building a secure digital future for our nation.
