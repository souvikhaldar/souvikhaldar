This is a continuation of the earlier [Crypto and Dark Web Forensics Training at CDTI](/events/cdti-crypto-darkweb-forensics-training/) post.

The course was held from September 2 to September 6, 2024 at Central Detective Training Institute, Hyderabad, and focused on modernizing investigative methods for crypto and dark web investigation.

During the session, I trained detectives and officers from intelligence and law-enforcement agencies on investigation workflows across artificial intelligence, cryptocurrency, blockchain, and dark web transactions. The program aligned with the Bureau of Police Research and Development's effort to strengthen investigative approaches for modern cybercrime.

It was an honour to be felicitated by DIG Rajshekara N, IPS, Director, CDTI, after training police officers from various states of India on cryptocurrency and dark web forensics.

Hornet is India's first indigenously researched and developed crypto and dark web forensics platform, built to help law-enforcement agencies solve complex crimes. National security has always been a prime concern, so Hornet focuses not only on solving cases but also on data integrity and security.

I am immensely thankful to the Bureau of Police Research and Development, Central Detective Training Institute, and Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, for trusting Hornet for this strategic collaboration.

Thanks to the entire Hornet team for building state-of-the-art software, and to The Bengal Chamber of Commerce and Industry, IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub at Indian Statistical Institute Kolkata, and STPI - Software Technology Parks of India for their unwavering support throughout the journey.

Join us in shaping the future of cyber warfare at Hornet.
