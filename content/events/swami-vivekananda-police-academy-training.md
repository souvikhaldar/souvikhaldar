I was invited to train officers at Swami Vivekananda State Police Academy on cybersecurity, crypto forensics, and dark web forensics.

The location made the experience especially meaningful: Barrackpore, where Mangal Pandey ignited the first sparks of India's freedom struggle in 1857.

Standing on such historic ground, it was a privilege to discuss modern cybersecurity challenges and the digital underworld with officers who protect us. The nature of threats has evolved, but the spirit of service remains unchanged.

Hornet continues to support law-enforcement training and investigation workflows around crypto-enabled crime, dark web intelligence, and cyber threats.
