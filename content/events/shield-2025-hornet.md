SHIELD 2025 became a landmark moment for Hornet.

Hon. CM Shri Revanth Reddy visited the Hornet booth and lauded Hornet's role in empowering law enforcement with indigenous crypto forensics and dark web intelligence.

Hornet was honoured to participate in SHIELD 2025, hosted by Telangana Cyber Security Bureau, Society for Cyberabad Security Council, and Cyberabad Police. The event brought together some of India's sharpest minds in law enforcement, intelligence, digital forensics, and cybercrime response.

At the event, Hornet showcased how it helps agencies trace illicit crypto trails, decode dark web patterns, and produce actionable compliance and investigation insights.

From engaging with officers from state and central police to discussing future readiness with senior IPS officers and cyber specialists, it was clear that India is preparing for the next generation of cybercrime response.

Hornet continues to be India's first fully indigenous crypto forensics and dark web intelligence platform, actively supporting multiple law-enforcement agencies and expanding globally.

Grateful to Shikha Goel IPS and Naved Khan for the invitation, trust, and encouragement.
