Hornet, the crypto forensics and compliance platform, was selected to be presented at RSA Conference 2024, held at Moscone Center in San Francisco from May 6 to May 9, 2024.

As Bitcoin and other digital assets grow, so do the risks of misuse through money laundering, ransomware, terrorism financing, fraud, and complex transaction chains. Hornet was built to help law-enforcement agencies and financial institutions investigate those patterns and meet regulatory expectations.

It was a pleasure to meet Michiel Prins, co-founder of HackerOne, and to receive collaboration and investment interest from Naveen Kumar of KFC Ventures. The experience also showed the strength of the Indian diaspora in global technology ecosystems.

Special thanks to Sandeep Kamble of SecureLayer7 for guidance, and to Consul General Melinda Pavek and The Bengal Chamber of Commerce and Industry for helping foster the innovation corridor.
