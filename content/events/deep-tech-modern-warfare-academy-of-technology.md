I was invited by the IEI Student Chapter, Department of EEE, Academy of Technology, to deliver a talk on "Deep Tech in Modern Warfare."

It felt refreshing to interact with young minds carrying curiosity, ambition, and the willingness to make an impact. Passionate young engineers, when guided well, can challenge the status quo and bring disruption to any domain. Often, all they need is a little push, awareness, and motivation.

Glad to be part of the technology revolution. A major upheaval is around the corner.
