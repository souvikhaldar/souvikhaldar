**Every major breach now ends in a wallet.** That was the message of my keynote at Crypto Expo Dubai. Grateful for the invitation and the opportunity to speak about the connection between cybersecurity, cryptocurrency investigations, and incident response.

Ransomware, exchange hacks, and business email compromise can leave investigators following money into crypto. The response needs to connect what happened during the breach with where the funds went afterwards.

My keynote focused on on-chain forensics as the missing last mile of incident response: moving from tracing transactions to attribution, freezing assets, and preparing evidence that holds up in court. Stopping at the perimeter or at a block explorer leaves that work unfinished.
