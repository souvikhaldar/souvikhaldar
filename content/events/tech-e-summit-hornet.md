Congratulations to Webel-BCC&I Tech Incubation Centre for successfully organizing the second edition of Tech-E Summit.

Hornet proved its mettle yet again, generating significant traction in the Silicon Valley of India.

It felt great to receive motivation from Shri Babul Supriyo, Minister for Information Technology and Electronics Department, Government of West Bengal. Support from ministers and institutions provides the much-needed boost to continue helping law-enforcement agencies with state-of-the-art crypto and dark web forensics technology.

The event brought together important stakeholders from DRDO, Ministry of Defence, Government of India, Information Technology Promotion Cell, Government of West Bengal, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, and Webel - West Bengal Electronics Industry Development Corporation Limited.
