# Six pillars of self confidence

Life lectures by Swami Sarvapriyananda:
1. Living consciously: It feels great after 3 hours of mindful work than 3 hours of internet browsing.
2. Acceptance of self: accept your flaws it is then the self development begins.
3. Live responsibly: Take responsibility for whatever is happening in your life.
4.  Be assertive: Assert what you want and don't be shy.
5. Live purposefully: Have a goal or purpose in life.
6. Self integrity: Have a standard for yourself of everything including honesty, truthfulness,etc.
