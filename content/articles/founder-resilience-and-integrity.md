Some periods test the difference between reputation, identity, and work.

Hornet was never just a company to me. It represented years of personal risk, engineering effort, difficult choices, and a belief that India can build serious technology for cybercrime investigation and financial security.

The recent period has been one of the hardest phases of my life. Allegations were made against me that I believe are baseless, and I have chosen to address them through the appropriate legal process. I continue to have faith that facts and truth will prevail.

What matters most is protecting the integrity of the work, the trust placed in us by officers and partners, and the effort that went into building technology with a bold mission.

Entrepreneurship is not only about growth, funding, and public milestones. It also asks for resilience when things become uncertain. I built with honesty, and I intend to move forward with dignity.
