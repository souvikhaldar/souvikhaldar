I joined a conversation with Dr. Sujoy Biswas of Techno India Group to discuss entrepreneurship, childhood, vision, risk-taking, and the journey behind Hornet.

Techno Main Salt Lake is my alma mater, so returning in a founder’s role carried a different emotional weight. The discussion connected personal history with the practical leap into building a technology company from Bengal.

The conversation was part of *Joyer Pathe Sange Sujoy*, an initiative highlighting stories, builders, and ideas connected to Bengal’s future in technology and entrepreneurship.
