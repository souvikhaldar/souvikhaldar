Hornet has a home now.

As a significant milestone, Hornet moved into a compact yet state-of-the-art office on the 11th floor of Mani Casadona in Kolkata on March 12, 2024.

Although the update came a little late, the team had been working tremendously hard toward the dream of protecting citizens who fall prey to digital currency related crimes and helping strengthen intelligence capabilities globally.

From working at home, to working at Webel-BCC&I Tech Incubation Centre, to finally moving into an office, this milestone became a testament to the commitment behind building a world-class cybersecurity product from Kolkata.

Hornet remains indebted to STPI - Software Technology Parks of India, Webel-BCC&I Tech Incubation Centre, Pontaq, and IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub at Indian Statistical Institute Kolkata for their continued support.

We are here to make an impact.
