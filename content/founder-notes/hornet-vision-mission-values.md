Hornet was built around a simple but demanding idea: investigation technology should help serious institutions fight cryptocurrency-enabled crime and dark-web threats with clarity, speed, and integrity.

The vision is to make advanced cyber and blockchain intelligence usable for the people who need it most: investigators, compliance teams, financial institutions, and public agencies working under real operational pressure.

The mission is practical. Hornet exists to turn complex digital trails into actionable intelligence: wallet tracing, entity discovery, dark-web monitoring, transaction-risk analysis, and reports that can support investigation and enforcement workflows.

The values behind that mission are equally important:

- Build with purpose, not vanity.
- Engineer deeply enough for high-stakes environments.
- Stay useful to investigators, not just impressive in demos.
- Treat trust, confidentiality, and operational integrity as product requirements.
- Keep learning from real cases and real users.

The original LinkedIn post/video framed these ideas as Hornet's vision, mission, and values.
