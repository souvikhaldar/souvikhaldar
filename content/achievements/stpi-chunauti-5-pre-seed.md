![Hornet wins STPI CHUNAUTI 5.0 pre-seed support](/images/linkedin/stpi-chunauti-5-pre-seed.jpg)

Hornet won STPI - Software Technology Parks of India CHUNAUTI 5.0 and received Rs 25 lakh in pre-seed support at an Rs 8 crore valuation from STPI in association with Pontaq.

The journey began with the CHUNAUTI 5.0 application on May 31, 2023, while Hornet was still in its early building phase. After nine months of product work, team-building, and fundraising discipline, the recognition became one of Hornet's most important early startup milestones.

The experience reinforced a hard founder lesson: building a startup demands patience, resilience, strong advisors, and the ability to stay focused through uncertainty. It also highlighted the value of institutions and mentors who support early-stage founders through funding, guidance, and ecosystem access.

Special thanks to STPI, Pontaq, The Bengal Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Webel-BCC&I Tech Incubation Centre, Radhav Tandon, Maneesh Kumar, Kalyan Kar, Arpan Pal, Deepak Daftari, Debashis Roy, Tanmoy Banerjee, Sambit Dasgupta, Rajat Dutta, Sagnik Dasgupta, and the mentors and supporters who helped shape this phase of Hornet's journey.
