A heartfelt thanks to The Bengal Chamber of Commerce and Industry for the honour of being felicitated.

The Bengal Chamber of Commerce and Industry has a legacy of more than 180 years in fostering economic development and innovation. It is one of the oldest business chambers in Asia.

I am deeply grateful to Webel-BCC&I Tech Incubation Centre for being a key part of my startup journey. Winning IDEATHON 6 in February 2023 led us to our first incubation program, where we received constant guidance, mentorship, and invaluable connections.

Special thanks to Sambit, who has been like an elder brother with advice and support; Angana, an amazing guide through every phase; and Rajat, like a younger brother, always full of energy and support.

I am forever thankful and look up to Arnab Basu. I hope to have them by my side through the journey ahead.
