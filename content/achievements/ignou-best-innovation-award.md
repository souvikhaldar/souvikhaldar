Hornet received the Best Innovation Award by IGNOU from Hon. Lt. Governor Delhi Shri Vinai Saxena.

The recognition marked an important milestone for the team and acknowledged Hornet's innovation, dedication, and early momentum in building technology for cybercrime investigation and intelligence.

Souvik Haldar represented Hornet at the award event as founder.
