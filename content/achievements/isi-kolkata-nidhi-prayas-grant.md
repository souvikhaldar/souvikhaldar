![Hornet receives ISI Kolkata NIDHI PRAYAS grant](/images/linkedin/isi-kolkata-nidhi-prayas-grant.jpg)

Hornet received a Rs 10 lakh grant from IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub at Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.

The support came through the IDEAS-TIH Cohort 2 program, funded by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, under the NIDHI PRAYAS Grant.

This recognition marked an important product and startup milestone for Hornet. It validated the potential of the team's work and helped accelerate development toward real-world cybercrime investigation and intelligence use cases.

Special thanks to Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub, Debashis Ghosh, Diptendu Dutta, Malay Bhattacharyya, Agnimitra Biswas, Kalyan Kar, and the mentors supporting innovation and entrepreneurship through the program.
