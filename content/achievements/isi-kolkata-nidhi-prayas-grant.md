![Hornet receives ISI Kolkata NIDHI PRAYAS grant](/images/linkedin/isi-kolkata-nidhi-prayas-grant.jpg)

Hornet was awarded a Rs 10 lakh grant by IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub at Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.

The support came through the IDEAS-TIH Cohort 2 program, funded by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, under the NIDHI PRAYAS Grant.

This recognition marked an important product and startup milestone for Hornet. It validated the team's hard work, dedication, and product potential, while helping accelerate development and bring the project closer to real-world use.

Hornet is committed to pushing the boundaries of technology and building systems that create meaningful impact. This grant became a significant milestone in that journey, giving the team renewed momentum to continue the work with focus and determination.

Thank you to Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata and IDEAS Technology Innovation Hub for believing in Hornet's vision and supporting innovation through the program.

Special thanks to Debashis Ghosh, Diptendu Dutta, Malay Bhattacharyya, Agnimitra Biswas, and Kalyan Kar for their commitment to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship.
