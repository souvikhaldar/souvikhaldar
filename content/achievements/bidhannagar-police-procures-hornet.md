Hornet won a significant procurement deal with Bidhannagar Police Commissionerate, strengthening its position in the fast-evolving crypto forensics industry.

This partnership is a game-changer. It reflects Hornet's ability to address the challenges created by rising cryptocurrency adoption and crypto-related crime.

Special thanks to Dr. Sonwane Kuldip Suresh, IPS, DCP, Detective Department, for recognizing Hornet's potential and leading this collaboration. Gratitude also to Commissioner Shri Mukesh, whose leadership and forward-looking approach continue to inspire.

This achievement is especially meaningful for three reasons:

1. Strategic importance: Bidhannagar Police covers the technology hub of Eastern India, mirroring Hornet's first breakthrough with Cyberabad Police in Telangana's technology hub.
2. Personal connection: both my home and Hornet's headquarters are located within this jurisdiction.
3. Proven results: Hornet has already supported Bidhannagar Police in solving major cryptocurrency-related cases, and the collaboration continues to make a real difference.

As the crypto industry grows, crimes involving digital assets are becoming increasingly sophisticated. Hornet is proud to empower law-enforcement agencies, including intelligence and anti-terrorism units, with tools to stay ahead of these challenges.

Hornet's vision is clear: shape the future of crypto crime detection and dark web investigations.
